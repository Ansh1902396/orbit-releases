The quiet art of designing less

By Aanya Rao · 6 min read

The best tools disappear. You stop noticing them the way you stop noticing a good chair — not because they do little, but because they never ask for your attention when they don’t need it.

Designing less is not removing features. It is removing every moment where a person has to think about the tool instead of the work: the extra tap, the confirmation nobody reads, the setting that should have been a sensible default.

Start with the one thing someone came to do, and make that effortless.
