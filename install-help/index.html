Installing Orbit

Blocked? One minute and you’re in.

Orbit isn’t on Google Play or the Mac App Store yet, so your phone and your Mac double‑check it first. Here’s how to get past each check — nothing here changes how safe your devices are once you’re done.

1“App blocked to protect your device” 2“Restricted setting” 3Mac: “Open Anyway”
Android phone

“App blocked to protect your device”

Google Play Protect · This app can request access to sensitive data. This can increase the risk of identity theft or financial fraud.

In some countries — India, Singapore, Thailand, Brazil and more — Google blocks every app installed from a browser that can show your notifications or texts somewhere else. That’s exactly what Orbit does (onto your Mac), so it’s caught too. Pause the check for a minute, install, then turn it straight back on.

  1. Tap OK on the message, then open the Play Store app.

  2. Tap your profile picture (top right), then Play Protect.

    Play Store›Profile picture›Play Protect

  3. Tap the settings gear (top right) and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. Confirm with Turn off.

    ⚙›Scan apps with Play Protect — off
    Some phones offer “Pause” instead — that works too.

  4. Open the Orbit file you downloaded again (it’s in Downloads, or tap the download notification) and tap Install.

    Didn’t keep it? Download it again below.

  5. Go back to Play Protect’s settings and turn Scan apps with Play Protect on again.

    Orbit stays installed and keeps working.

Android phone · while setting up

“Restricted setting”

Restricted setting · For your security, this setting is currently unavailable.

Android shows this the first time Orbit asks to show your notifications on the Mac or to help you copy. It’s a one‑time unlock for apps installed from the web.

  1. Tap OK. (Android only offers the unlock after you’ve seen this message once.)

  2. Open Settings › Apps › Orbit.

    Settings›Apps›Orbit

  3. Tap ⋮ (top right), then Allow restricted settings, and confirm with your fingerprint or PIN.

    ⋮›Allow restricted settings

  4. Go back to Orbit and turn the switch on again — it works now.

Mac · first launch

“Orbit Not Opened”

“Orbit” Not Opened · Apple could not verify “Orbit” is free of malware…

Your Mac asks this once for apps from outside the App Store.

  1. Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security.

    Apple menu›System Settings›Privacy & Security

  2. Scroll down to Security. Next to “Orbit was blocked…”, click Open Anyway.

    Open Anyway

  3. Enter your Mac password, then click Open Anyway once more. Orbit opens — and from now on it opens normally.

Questions

Is it safe to turn off Play Protect for this?

For the minute it takes to install Orbit, yes — just turn it back on afterwards, and only install apps you trust this way. Play Protect keeps checking your other apps as before once it’s back on.

Why does Orbit need these permissions at all?

To show your phone’s notifications on your Mac (and let you reply), Orbit needs notification access. To send what you copy on your phone to your Mac, and to let you use your phone from your Mac, it uses Accessibility. Everything goes straight between your phone and your Mac — never through anyone’s servers.

Will I have to do this for every update?

No. Orbit updates itself from inside the app. If Android ever blocks an update the same way, repeat the steps above.

Will this go away?

Yes — we’re working on making Orbit available in places where these checks don’t apply, so installing is a single tap.
