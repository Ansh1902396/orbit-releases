Questions

Good to know.

Is Orbit free?

Yes. Orbit is free during the beta, with no ads and no account.

Which phones and Macs does it work with?

Any phone running Android 10 or later, and any Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Does my data go through a server?

No. Your phone and your Mac talk to each other directly on your network, encrypted end to end. There’s no Orbit cloud.

Can I talk through my Mac during a call?

Not yet — today you can see, answer, decline and dial calls from your Mac while the conversation stays on your phone. Call audio on your Mac is next on our list.

Why does macOS ask me to confirm the first launch?

The beta isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks once. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose “Open Anyway”. You won’t see it again.
